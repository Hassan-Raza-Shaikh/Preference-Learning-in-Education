## A. Information sheet for parents / carers

**Study title:** [Personalised maths practice with a computer tutor: a pilot study]

**Who is running it:** [Name], [degree programme], [university], supervised by [supervisor
name]. Contact: [email].

**What is the study about?** We are testing a computer tutor that helps pupils practise
solving pairs of equations. It offers different kinds of help (a worked example, the first
steps done, a reminder of the method, or no help) and learns which kind of help suits each
pupil. Every calculation it shows has been checked by computer, so it does not show wrong
maths.

**What would my child do?** In [number] maths lessons of about [30] minutes, your child
would practise on the tutor in class, with their teacher present. They would also do a
short quiz at the start and end (about 10 minutes each). Pupils are placed by chance into
one of three versions of the tutor, which differ only in how help is chosen.

**What is recorded?** A code (not your child's name), which problems they did, which help
they chose or were given, whether their answers were right, how long they took, and how
helpful they found the help (1–3). We do **not** record names, photos, voice, location or
anything your child types other than the numbers in their answers. Only the teacher keeps
the list linking codes to names; the researchers never see it.

**Is it compulsory?** No. Taking part is voluntary and does not affect your child's
marks. You can withdraw your child at any time, without giving a reason, until [date when
data are anonymised]. After that date the data cannot be linked to your child, so they
cannot be removed. Pupils who do not take part will do [the same practice without data
being kept / their usual class work].

**Risks and benefits.** Risks are minimal: it is ordinary maths practice. A possible benefit
is extra practice with help suited to your child. The results will help design better
learning tools.

**What happens to the results?** They will be written up in a thesis and possibly a
research paper, using group results only. No pupil or school will be identifiable.
Data are kept as described in the data-management plan and deleted by [date].

**Questions or complaints:** [researcher email]; [supervisor email]; [ethics office contact].

## C. Pupil assent (read aloud by the teacher; age-appropriate)

> We are trying out a computer tutor that helps with maths. If you want to, you will use it
> in a few lessons and do two short quizzes. The tutor will ask what kind of help you like,
> and it will try to find what helps you most.
>
> It is **not** a test of you, and it does **not** count for your marks. The computer does
> not know your name, only a code. If you don't want to take part, or want to stop at any
> time, that is completely fine. Just tell your teacher, and you won't get into any trouble.
>
> Do you have any questions? Would you like to take part?

Pupil's choice (circle): **Yes, I'd like to try it** / **No, thank you**

## D. Teacher information (short)

You will hand out learner codes and keep the code↔name list securely; it never leaves the
school. You will open the quiz at the start and end, and supervise practice. The teacher
page shows each pupil's progress, condition and quiz scores. Please report any suspected
maths error or technical problem to [researcher] the same day. You can stop the study at
any time.
